01 — CAPABILITIES

Five processes, one supplier, one point of contact.

Machining, casting, forming, joining and finishing happen in the same plant — so tolerances are held across process boundaries instead of across suppliers.

CNC Machining

Over 60 machines including five-axis centres and mill-turn cells. Complex geometry is finished in one setup, which removes the stack-up error that repeated re-fixturing introduces.

  • Machines60+ incl. 5-axis, mill-turn
  • Typical tolerance±0.01 mm
  • In-process controlOn-machine measurement
  • Volumes1 piece → series

Swiss-Type Turning

More than ten multi-axis sliding-headstock lathes for small, slender, high-count parts — the geometry where a standard lathe loses accuracy as the part deflects.

  • Machines10+ multi-axis
  • Best forSmall precision components
  • BatchHigh-count series

Aluminium Die Casting

Established tooling and process control for dimensionally stable aluminium castings, machined in-house afterwards so the casting and the finished part come from one supplier.

  • MaterialAluminium alloys
  • ToolingIn-house die support
  • Post-processCNC machined in-house

Sheet Metal Fabrication

A flexible sheet metal line covering cutting, forming and bending — suited to enclosures and brackets that accompany machined assemblies.

  • LineFlexible, automated
  • OperationsCut · form · bend

Welding

Robotic welding and weld repair. Automating the joint keeps heat input and bead geometry consistent from the first part to the last, which is where manual welding drifts.

  • ProcessRobotic welding
  • AlsoWeld repair

Surface Finishing

An automated powder coating line plus supporting surface treatments, applied in-house so finished parts ship without an extra vendor in the chain.

  • LineAutomated powder coating
  • HandoverNo external finisher
