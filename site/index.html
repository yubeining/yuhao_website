Capabilities

Five manufacturing processes, one point of contact.

60+ CNC machines and 10+ Swiss-type lathes across a 15,000 m² plant in Jiangsu, China.

CNC Machining

Over 60 machines including five-axis centres and mill-turn cells. Complex geometry is finished in fewer setups, which is where repeat-fixturing error normally creeps in.

  • Machines60+ incl. 5-axis, mill-turn
  • In-process controlOn-machine measurement
  • MaterialsAluminium · steel · stainless
  • Volume1 piece to series

Swiss-Type Turning

More than ten multi-axis sliding-headstock lathes for small, slender, high-count parts — the geometry where a conventional lathe loses accuracy as the workpiece deflects.

  • Machines10+ multi-axis
  • Best forSmall precision components
  • VolumeHigh-count series

Aluminium Die Casting

Established tooling and process control for dimensionally stable aluminium castings — then machined in the same plant, so the casting and the finished part come from one supplier.

  • MaterialAluminium alloys
  • ToolingIn-house die support
  • Post-processCNC machined in-house

Sheet Metal Fabrication

A flexible sheet metal line covering cutting, forming and bending — for the enclosures and brackets that ship alongside machined assemblies.

  • LineFlexible, automated
  • OperationsCut · form · bend

Welding

Robotic welding and weld repair. Automating the joint keeps the bead consistent from the first part to the last — the point where manual welding tends to drift.

  • ProcessRobotic welding
  • AlsoWeld repair

Surface Finishing

An automated powder coating line and supporting surface treatments, applied in-house so finished parts ship without another vendor in the chain.

  • LineAutomated powder coating
  • HandoverNo external finisher

Quality

Measured, recorded, traceable.

ISO 13485 means our quality system was audited against medical device requirements. That documentation discipline applies to every order, not only medical parts.

  • Incoming material check. Certificates verified and recorded against the batch.
  • On-machine measurement. Features probed in the fixture, before the part is released.
  • CMM verification. Coordinate measuring machine in a dedicated inspection room.
  • Documented handover. Every step recorded, so any dimension can be traced back.
